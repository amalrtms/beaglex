How evolution learned to trade

Nobody programmed these strategies. Each one is a set of ten numbers, its DNA: how much to follow trends, how big to trade, how often to act, whether to quote both sides. Twenty random strategies trade together for four days; the most profitable survive and breed, mixing and slightly mutating their DNA. Repeat for 100 generations.

Within a few generations every run converges on the same answer: market making, the strategy real trading firms use. Then the market makers compete with each other, pushing the spread down to one cent and their own profits with it. Drag the slider or press play to watch it happen.
